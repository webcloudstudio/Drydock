We are pleased to announce a new Drydock update — **version 0.8 Beta**. In this version we
introduce several major features including:

- **Acceptance Criteria Testing Framework.** A Verify and Repair Loop On Acceptance Criteria.
  The Builder Loops to create correct Software. **Acceptance Criteria define the Definition of Done.**
- **Full UAT Harness.** **10 projects, scored on Haiku and Luna.** Tests the complete delivery
  lifecycle.
- **Generated database layer.** Encapsulated persistence for **Python and Go**, supporting SQLite
  and PostgreSQL.
- **Diagnosis and repair.** Finds root causes, corrects Blueprints, and rebuilds affected work.
- **Controlled change.** REFIT tickets turn change requests into targeted builds.
- **Model choice and traceability.** **Claude, Codex, and Gemini.** Track specifications through
  code, tests, and delivery evidence.

**Use Drydock to make “done” executable: specify the behavior, build it, verify it, and repair
what fails.**
