Meet Drydock: a complete software delivery system for specification-driven applications.

Drydock works with larger specifications. Multi-file specifications, and imports from other tools.

Drydock import brings in specifications, notes, and other material.
(pause)

Drydock analyze proposes an Agile plan. Stories, questions, blockers, and acceptance criteria.
(pause)

Drydock plan converts Stories into Blueprints. Each Blueprint carries Test-Driven Development assertions.

Plan also builds the Manifest: a graph database relating Blueprints, Branding, and enterprise stack rules.
(pause)

You steer the build in the QuarterDeck web server. Group your stories, and optimize the context.

Drydock build walks the Manifest, verifying each step against predefined tests.
(pause)

Change tickets are conformed with drydock refit, and delivered with drydock build.
